### Mermaid diagrams accept mermaid 12

`@fumadocs-editor/ui` now depends on `mermaid ^11.17.2 || ^12.2.0`. A site
that renders its own diagrams with mermaid 12 can install the same copy for
the editor, so diagrams look the same in the editor and on the site. The
editor only calls `mermaid.initialize` and `mermaid.render`, which mermaid 12
keeps. Projects that stay on mermaid 11 are unaffected.
